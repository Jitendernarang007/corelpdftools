Privacy

Draft — review before launch.

  • Files you upload are processed automatically to make a CorelDRAW-compatible copy.
  • Your original file is deleted as soon as processing finishes. The fixed file and its previews are deleted within 1 hour.
  • We do not look at, share, sell or reuse your files.
  • If you click “Did it open?”, we store your answer and the CorelDRAW version you picked, linked only to a random job number.
  • We count how many files each internet address fixes per day to enforce the free limit. This count is not stored permanently.
